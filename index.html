Site tour

Welcome

❓ How to use this site

The Signature Global Newspaper Archive prints a fresh edition from each of six papers every morning, reporting real events from the 27-site Signature network — drip milestones, fixes shipped, launches and records. Early archive editions are retired Signature-world fiction, clearly labeled. No real-world news, no real persons. Here is what every button and box does, in plain language.

📰 Today's editions

The top cards are the newest editions — one per paper. Tap any card to open that full edition.

🔎 Search

  • Type words to search headlines, or an edition ID like JAH-ED-002190 to jump straight to it.
  • The scope menu next to the search box switches between Headlines + IDs, Article text (searches the full story text of every article), and both.
  • Find it (the top finder bar) takes a plain description — e.g. "a lantern festival" — and returns the five best-matching editions.

🗂 Filters & browsing

  • All six papers menu: show one paper only, or all of them.
  • Year / Month / date: pick a date and press Go to date to see what printed that day. (Papers print every morning; if a date has no editions the page says so plainly.)
  • Latest: jumps back to the newest editions. Load more editions: shows older editions, 24 at a time.

📖 Reading an edition

An edition opens like a broadsheet: masthead, date, volume and issue, then every story with its section, headline, byline and full text. Buttons under each story copy or download that single article.

❓ Ask about this edition

The Ask-AI box at the bottom of every edition answers questions using only that edition's printed stories — headlines, summaries, bylines, sections. Tap a suggestion chip or type your own question. It always answers; if something isn't printed here it says so.

🔊 Read aloud · 📋 Copy · ⬇ Download

  • 🔊 Read edition aloud: the whole edition is read out loud, chunk by chunk, with a stop button.
  • 📋 Copy edition: copies the full edition text so you can paste it anywhere.
  • ⬇ Download .txt / .json: saves the edition as a plain-text or data file on your device.
  • 📖 Reading mode: switches the broadsheet to a single comfortable reading column.

🔗 Deep links

Every edition, article and world entry has its own link you can bookmark or share:

  • ?edition=JAH-ED-002190 — opens that exact edition.
  • ?article=JAH-ARTICLE-013140 — opens the edition scrolled to that article.
  • ?verify=JAH-ED-002190 — recomputes the edition's SHA-256 hash and proves the record is intact.
  • ?entity=JAH-ENTITY-000001 — a fictional Signature-world person, place or organization and where it appeared.

ℹ️ About the papers

The About the papers section lists all six papers with their regions and taglines; Fresh off the press shows today's headlines for crawlers and no-script readers.
